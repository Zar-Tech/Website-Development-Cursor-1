About VitalTools

VitalTools is a lightweight health utility website template built for founders who want to launch quickly on Hostinger and monetize with AdSense.

What makes this template useful?

  • Fast static pages with mobile-first design.
  • Calculator-specific pages optimized for search intent.
  • Trust pages (privacy + disclaimer) required for ad approval.
  • Theme switcher for visual style testing before launch.
